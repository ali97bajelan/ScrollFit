# T14 phone checks — all pending

Intended device: physical Android 14 phone. No device result is claimed.
Build/install a debug candidate when official Android artifacts/SDK are available.
Record actual results, locale, font scale, TalkBack state and observed failures.

1. Fresh launch: review purpose/privacy/defaults and limitations, open setup,
   return and enter Home. Relaunch and rotate: onboarding completion persists.
   Settings → Setup and privacy information reopens the explanation.
2. Select two safe launchable apps. Search by name/package; clear the query and
   confirm selections remain. Essential/system/launcher/keyboard apps are absent.
   Save, restart and confirm selected apps and separate settings persist.
3. Enter Persian, Arabic and ASCII digits. Free allowance/award cap accept zero;
   ceiling/session/repetition/reward reject zero, negatives, decimals, signs and
   overflow. Each invalid field explains correction; saving remains disabled.
4. Change each app's allowance/cap, combined ceiling/session boundary and zero-base
   mode. Existing consumption, unused credit, total awarded and partial/pending
   sessions remain. Cap zero stops new rewards but retains old credit.
5. Grant Usage Access/Accessibility then return directly to setup. Health and
   enablement update; enabling requires a selectable target and access. Revoke
   access and confirm actionable missing-permission status. Stop monitoring from
   Settings and confirm service/overlay behavior and persisted disablement.
6. Disable/uninstall a configured app while setup is backgrounded; return.
   Unavailable state appears and unrelated saves preserve its stored selection.
   Remove it explicitly, save and re-enable/reinstall/re-add: prior counters remain.
   Restore/install an app while setup is backgrounded and confirm list refreshes.
7. Home: compare each displayed metric with known consumption and awards. Earn
   preselects the correct app. App exhaustion, combined exhaustion and award-cap
   messages explain the distinct restrictions. Exercise-only still opens at caps.
   Opening an unavailable target reports failure without losing credit/history.
8. Settings: edit all four repetition/reward rules, speech, camera and next
   exercise. Rotate with unsaved exercise preferences; drafts remain. Save/restart
   and confirm the catalog/challenge preparation uses the saved values. Existing
   challenges keep captured terms. Unedited fields preserve concurrent changes.
9. Complete each exercise from the catalog and confirm saved exercise-only
   activity with no credit. Earn from Home/blocker, complete and return: actual
   committed reward and updated Home/history appear. Camera denial/errors offer
   recovery; backgrounding/cancel stops camera and speech without partial awards.
10. History: inspect empty state, usage-only days, exercise-only activity, rewarded
    challenges and removed-app records. Confirm duration/repetitions/awarded time
    remain distinct and no missing period is described as zero usage or progress loss.
11. Delete history: dismiss confirmation first and verify records remain; confirm
    deletion and verify visible records disappear while today's budgets, award
    totals/capacity, pending sessions and duplicate-award protection stay unchanged.
    Restart; existing-day history stays hidden after further spending. New-day
    records appear normally. Retrying a completed challenge cannot grant again.
12. Switch Persian/English/French; test RTL/LTR, mixed package names/numbers,
    200% font scale, landscape/small screen, light/dark contrast and scrolling.
    TalkBack announces selection labels, roles/states and field errors; controls
    have 48 dp targets, navigation symbols are not read as duplicate labels.
13. Keep Home/history visible across local midnight and return from another app:
    today's figures refresh without mixed dates, stale credit or an allowance reset
    caused by navigation. Storage/read failure displays retry rather than empty data.

These checks supplement T05/T11–T13 acceptance; they do not replace them. Weekly
outcomes/coverage checks belong to T15 and integrated APK delivery belongs to T16.
