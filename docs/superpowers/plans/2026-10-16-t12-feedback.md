# T12 spoken counting and feedback implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task by task.

**Goal:** Give live challenges optional offline spoken counts, sustained-loss prompts, and saved-result announcements, with sound and visible fallback.

**Architecture:** An Android-free feedback controller owns deduplication, one active/one latest pending message, expiry, warning cooldown and interruption. A screen-owned Android adapter selects an installed offline voice matching the UI locale, translates semantic messages, and otherwise plays short local tones. ChallengeFlow feeds accepted progress and persisted results and stops output synchronously on interruption.

**Tech stack:** Existing Kotlin/JUnit, Android TextToSpeech, ToneGenerator, Compose, existing DataStore speechEnabled.

**Spec:** exercise_gate_tasks.md T12; exercise_gate_codex_brief.md required convenience features.

## Review focus

- Late initialization/completion callbacks after cancellation must remain silent.
- Muting clears all output; enabling again never replays earlier events.
- Rapid counts coalesce; old pending counts expire after 1 second.
- Tracking loss is sustained for 2 seconds and prompts are at least 10 seconds apart, including loss/recovery flapping.
- A reduced award is announced from CompletionResult.Awarded.amountMs only; refused completion never announces success.
