## Acceptance steps

1. Open Monitoring setup in Persian, English, and French. At a narrow width and large text size, confirm the daily free time, earned time, daily reward cap, and shared daily limit remain distinguishable and readable.
2. Enable monitoring, then inspect the connected status and limitations notice. Confirm the connected status does not promise that every app use will be recorded or blocked.
3. Remove a required access or stop the monitoring service. Confirm the status explains that limits may not be active and directs the person to check monitoring status and recorded usage.
4. Reach an app budget limit and the shared daily limit separately. Confirm each screen names the correct limit, that exercise-only mode remains available when shown, and that earned time is described accurately.
5. In a controlled run that reaches a camera model error, confirm the challenge reports the interruption with no partial award. Confirm a pending cancellation disables the new-attempt action; if saving fails, Retry is available and a new attempt stays unavailable until the save succeeds. Then review the current terms and start a fresh challenge. Confirm App info opens Android’s ScrollFit app details and returning leaves existing earned time unchanged.
6. Check split-screen, picture-in-picture, service restart, and the device’s battery/background restrictions. Record observations by device; do not generalize one manufacturer’s behavior to all Android phones.
