## I05 scenario coverage

The four JVM coordinator tests passed for fail-once retry, a retry burst, disable during suspended initialization, and preserving the existing budget/gap across retry. The Android fixture `MonitoringServiceLifecycleTest.retriesBootFailureWithoutCreatingASecondConsumerOrLease` did not execute.

The separate boot identity, persistence, and metadata fail-once cases; service connection/consumer counts; real stop/destroy behavior during suspended storage; and persistence-backed recovery were not injected or observed on a device. I05-01 and I05-03 retain required Android cases, and the Android fixture cannot compile until the shared app test-source errors are fixed. The Home launch smoke showed the app remained in its default disabled state; no service or special permission was enabled.

I05 is **BLOCKED**, not accepted on JVM coverage alone. Rerun the Android service fixture and the device failure-injection cases after the app Android-test sources compile.
